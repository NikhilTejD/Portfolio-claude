Digital governance2025 – now

Rescuing a stalled WhatsApp governance rollout

12% → 31%adoption across ~10,000 citizens in 3 secretariats

Organic campaigns had flatlined. I diagnosed why, redesigned the rollout and other supervisors copied the approach.

Full case
Situation
Mana Mitra moved citizen services onto WhatsApp. Banners, volunteers and operator nudges left adoption stuck at 12% while administrators pushed for numbers.
What I did
Interviewed citizen segments and mapped resistance with ADKAR: it broke at Desire and Ability (fear of costly mistakes, an ageing population, distrust of soft-copy documents). Rationed offline capacity toward elderly citizens and set up trainer-staffed self-service stalls beside operators.
Result
Adoption rose to 31%. Supervisors in other locations replicated the stall model independently.

Housing · APSHCL2021 – 2023

Unblocking the only stalled housing site in the state

125beneficiaries building inside the payment window, all milestones paid

A public promise by a local MP froze construction. I reframed the problem around the payment deadline and moved each group on its own terms.

Full case
Situation
With land, borewell and power under way, the MP pledged to fund full third-party construction, then withdrew. Beneficiaries refused to build, media amplified it, and this became the one stalled location on a central scheme.
What I did
Separated the real risk (a fixed government release window) from the public dispute. Segmented beneficiaries by what would move each group and worked them in parallel for a month, with the District Collector in the loop.
Result
Every beneficiary started inside the window. All milestone payments completed, with RWS, power and PR follow-through.

Emergency response2021 flood

A washed-out bridge and an overnight road

24 hrsto restore access for a 250-house habitation

Ran ground command through two flood crises in one week, including an Indian Air Force helicopter rescue.

Full case
Situation
A 60 m bridge partly collapsed, cutting off the only route to ~250 homes. Days later a car and a JCB carrying six people were stranded mid-bridge in rising water.
What I did
Dropped a drone plan that wasn't working, mobilised JCBs, labour and villagers to cut a 2 km temporary track overnight, and regularised spend afterwards. For the rescue, escalated past my authority to the Collector and MLA, with the CM's office fast-tracking IAF approval and divers on standby downstream.
Result
Access restored in about a day. All six people extracted safely.

Samagra Shiksha2020 – 2021

Turning an SOP dead end into a state-approved process

1 G.O.issued to allow resource transfer across projects

Two school projects were each stuck for opposite reasons. Moving cement between them was the fix, and no rule allowed it.

Full case
Situation
Post-COVID payment delays left one school with surplus cement and no builder, another with a builder and no cement. Cement expires in about three months.
What I did
Escalated past an unwilling AE to the Deputy and Executive Engineers, secured a Government Order with guidelines, and drafted an MoU between both school committees to lend and replenish stock.
Result
No material wasted, both projects moving. It changed how I treat SOPs: as guardrails I can improve with the right approvals.

Why rides fail: Uber NCR fulfilment

18%driver cancellations, the largest single cause of a 62% completion rate

Tested five hypotheses through segmentation and root-cause analysis, then built dashboards and recommendations.

Full case
Question
What is holding ride completion at 62%?
Method
Broke failures down by cause, pickup delay band, location, time of day and vehicle type.
Finding
Cancellations peak near 25% in the 5–10 minute pickup band, concentrated in specific zones. Time of day and vehicle type barely mattered.
Recommendations
Driver pre-positioning in high-delay zones, incentives targeted at 5–10 minute pickups, and smarter matching.
